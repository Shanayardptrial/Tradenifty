# TradeNifty Pro - TradingView Institutional Overlay (Chrome Extension)

Real-time institutional radar overlay for **TradingView** (`https://in.tradingview.com/chart/`).

## 🚀 Features Plotted Directly on TradingView:
1. **🦈 Institutional Shark Walls**:
   - **Shark Call Wall (Major Resistance)**: Strike with highest institutional call writing.
   - **Shark Put Support (Major Floor)**: Strike with highest institutional put writing.
2. **📊 Open Interest (OI) & Max Pain**:
   - Dynamic Max Pain Strike Ray Line & Label.
   - Dynamic ATM Strike Ray Line & Label.
3. **🎯 Live AI Algorithmic Trade Signals**:
   - Floating Glassmorphic HUD with active contract (`BUY CALL` or `BUY PUT`).
   - Trigger Price, Target 1, Stop Loss, and AI Confidence.
   - On-Chart Target & SL Lines.
4. **🏷️ Right Price-Axis Sticky Badges**:
   - Pinned along the TradingView right price scale for instant strike distance readability.
5. **📑 Mini 7-Strike Option Chain Drawer**:
   - Instant expandable option chain popup directly on TradingView without switching tabs.

---

## 🛠️ How to Install in Google Chrome:
1. Open Google Chrome and go to: `chrome://extensions/`
2. Turn ON **"Developer mode"** (top-right toggle switch).
3. Click on the button **"Load unpacked"** (top-left).
4. Select this folder:
   `c:\Users\Admin\Desktop\Sipara\Tradenifty\Extention`
5. Open any TradingView Chart:
   [https://in.tradingview.com/chart/](https://in.tradingview.com/chart/)
6. The TradeNifty HUD and price plots will automatically appear live on your chart!

---

## ⚙️ Configuration & Settings:
- Click the **TradeNifty Pro** icon in your Chrome toolbar to test local server connectivity (`http://127.0.0.1:5000`), toggle lines/badges, adjust refresh rate, or toggle sound alerts.
